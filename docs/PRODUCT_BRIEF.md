# Dream Life App: Product Brief

## One line
A faith-rooted life tracker that helps you see who you're becoming, check whether you're actually showing up for it, and keep the proof of your growth in one place.

## Who it's for
Women who are ambitious and faith-driven (entrepreneurs, creators, coaching clients) who want more than a habit tracker: a place to dream, plan, track and celebrate across every area of life.

## The problem
Goal-setting tools are either cold productivity apps or pretty vision boards with no follow-through. Nothing connects the dream (who I want to be in 5 years) to the daily reality (did I show up today?) with warmth and faith built in.

## The solution
1. **Dream**: 1, 3 and 5 year vision boards (Dream You).
2. **Plan**: one goal and next action for each of 10 life areas.
3. **Track**: 30-second daily check-in, weekly reset, monthly life score.
4. **Celebrate**: win board, highlight reel, and a place to let go of what no longer serves you.

## Differentiators
- Life score across 10 areas, not just habits.
- Faith woven in naturally ("Where I saw God move").
- A win board built for tough days.
- Distinct, playful brand (cream, lime, serif, scrolling ticker).

## Pricing (draft, to validate)
- Free: daily check-in, life score, goals, win board.
- Premium (monthly or annual): Dream You boards, highlights with photos, weekly insights, AI review queue, guided visualizations, AI path to your vision.

## Phases
1. **Validate (2 to 4 weeks)**: run the web prototype with 10 to 20 students and followers, and sell founding-member access.
2. **MVP app (iOS + Android)**: daily, overview, monthly, goals, win board, accounts, subscriptions.
3. **Premium**: Dream You, highlights, weekly reset, notifications.
4. **AI layer**: review queue, AI path to your vision, guided voice visualizations, wearable and calendar integrations.

## Launch checklist
- LLC, Apple Developer account ($99/yr, D-U-N-S number for a company), Google Play account ($25 once)
- Apply for the App Store Small Business Program
- Privacy policy and terms (reviewed by a lawyer)
- TestFlight / Play closed-testing beta
- Store screenshots, description, preview video
- Waitlist built from TikTok, YouTube and the coaching community

## Success metrics
- Day-7 and day-30 retention
- Daily check-ins per active user per week
- Free to premium conversion
- Monthly check-in completion rate
